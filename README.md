One sentence of horror for the [One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488) bounty on make.fun.

The entry is a single line, under 200 characters: a phone lights up at 3:12 a.m. with a photo of its owner asleep, taken from the foot of the bed. The fright is in what the sentence never says — who was standing there in the dark, holding the phone, while its owner slept.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
